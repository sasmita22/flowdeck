## Standard

The authoritative text of the standard is the `## Standard` section of `.claude/skills/audit-ai-guidelines/SKILL.md`; this list only names the rules as audited. Every skill, agent and prompt meets all seven:

1. One concern per skill.
2. The description says what and when.
3. The body is short enough to read in full (at most 200 lines).
4. Output shapes are pinned.
5. Stop conditions are explicit.
6. No rule beyond what the blueprint asks.
7. Wording is consistent across skills.

Rules 2 and 3, the name matching its folder and the absence of ticket ids are checked mechanically by `test/kit/skill-standard.test.ts` over every `kit` and `kit-blueprint` skill, both kit agents and the audit skill itself. The other rules are judged by the audit.
